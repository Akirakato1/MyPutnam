The locked PDFs and TeX files remain under `content-src/<year>/original/` for development verification only. No PDF or TeX source belongs beneath `app/src/main/assets`, and this source-batch freeze performs no release aggregation or APK assembly.

The ignored `dist/` directory still contains only the pre-plan `putnam-workbook-2025-v0.3.0.apk` and its checksum, both timestamped 2026-10-02 UTC; this plan did not produce a new distribution artifact.
