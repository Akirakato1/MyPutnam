1. Pair all nonzero strings by flipping their first nonzero digit between 1 and 2.
  2. Verify that the pairing is an involution and each vertex is adjacent to its partner.
  3. After every Alice move, have Bob move to the paired vertex; prove by induction that this vertex is unused.
  4. Use finiteness to show the game must end, and observe that Bob is never the first player without a response.
