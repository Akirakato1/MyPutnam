Problem A6

Alice and Bob play a game in which they take turns choosing integers from 111 to nnn. Before any integers are chosen, Bob selects a goal of "odd" or "even". On the first turn, Alice chooses one of the nnn integers. On the second turn, Bob chooses one of the remaining integers. They continue alternately choosing an integer that has not yet been chosen, until the forced nnnth turn ends the game.

Bob wins if the parity of

{k:k was chosen on turn k}\{k:k\text{ was chosen on turn }k\}{k:k was chosen on turn k}

matches his goal. For which values of nnn does Bob have a winning strategy?
