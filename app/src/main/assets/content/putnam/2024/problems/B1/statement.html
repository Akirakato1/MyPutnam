Problem B1

Let nnn and kkk be positive integers. The square in the iiith row and jjjth column of an nnn-by-nnn grid contains the number i+j−ki+j-ki+j−k.

For which nnn and kkk is it possible to select nnn squares from the grid, no two in the same row or column, such that the numbers contained in the selected squares are exactly 1,2,…,n1,2,\ldots,n1,2,…,n?
