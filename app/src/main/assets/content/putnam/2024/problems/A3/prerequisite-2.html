The hook-length formula counts standard tableaux. In the three-row case it simplifies to

f(u,v,w)=u−v+1(u+2)!u−w+2(v+1)!⋅(v−w+1)(u+v+w)!w!.\begin{aligned} f(u,v,w)&=\frac{u-v+1}{(u+2)!}\frac{u-w+2}{(v+1)!}\\ &\quad\cdot\frac{(v-w+1)(u+v+w)!}{w!}. \end{aligned}f(u,v,w)​=(u+2)!u−v+1​(v+1)!u−w+2​⋅w!(v−w+1)(u+v+w)!​.​

It can also be proved directly by induction, placing the largest entry at the right end of one row and using the resulting three-term recurrence.
