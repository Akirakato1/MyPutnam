Relabeling vertices only permutes the four side lengths, while the recorded opposite-angle sum is one of θ\thetaθ and 2π−θ2\pi-\theta2π−θ. Therefore every member of a partner sequence is represented by one of finitely many quintuples formed from the original four side lengths and those two angle sums.

If the sequence were infinite, two members would share a representing quintuple. By the uniqueness just proved, those two quadrilaterals would be congruent, contradicting the requirement. Thus the answer is no.
