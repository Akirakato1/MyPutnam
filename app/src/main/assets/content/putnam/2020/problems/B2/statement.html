Problem B2

Let kkk and nnn be integers with 1≤k<n1\le k<n1≤k<n. Alice and Bob play a game with kkk pegs in a line of nnn holes. At the beginning of the game, the pegs occupy the kkk leftmost holes. A legal move consists of moving a single peg to any vacant hole that is further to the right. The players alternate moves, with Alice playing first. The game ends when the pegs are in the kkk rightmost holes, so whoever is next to play cannot move and therefore loses. For what values of nnn and kkk does Alice have a winning strategy?
