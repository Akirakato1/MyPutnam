Problem A1

A grasshopper starts at the origin in the coordinate plane and makes a sequence of hops. Each hop has length 555, and after each hop the grasshopper is at a point whose coordinates are both integers; thus, there are 121212 possible locations for the grasshopper after the first hop. What is the smallest number of hops needed for the grasshopper to reach the point (2021,2021)(2021,2021)(2021,2021)?
