Problem B3

Assign to each positive real number a color, either red or blue. Let DDD be the set of all distances d>0d>0d>0 for which there are two points of the same color at distance ddd apart. Recolor the positive reals so that the numbers in DDD are red and the numbers not in DDD are blue. If this recoloring process is iterated, will all the numbers always become red after finitely many steps?
